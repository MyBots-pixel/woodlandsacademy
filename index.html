What guides us

Three values. One community.

Our values shape the way we learn, work together and contribute to school life every day.

01 / BE READY

Come prepared

Bring the right attitude, equipment and willingness to take on new challenges.

02 / BE RESPECTFUL

Treat people well

Listen, show kindness, celebrate differences and help everyone feel that they belong.

03 / BE RESPONSIBLE

Make good choices

Take pride in your actions, look after our shared environment and keep moving forward.
